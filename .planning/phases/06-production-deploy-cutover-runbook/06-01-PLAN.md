ASVS V9.1.1. Inline comment at HSTS line cites DEPLOY-06 to block PR-review regression to preload. |
| T-06-V9-XFO | Tampering | Frame embedding (clickjacking on legal pages, hero, form) | mitigate | `X-Frame-Options: DENY` emitted on every response (D-11). Universal browser support including IE 11+; complements future CSP `frame-ancestors`. ASVS V9.2.x. |
| T-06-V9-NS | Spoofing | MIME sniffing on user-agent-rendered content | mitigate | `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff` (D-11). Defense-in-depth — no uploads exist, but blocks any future regression where a binary blob serves with a wrong content-type. ASVS V9.3.x. |
